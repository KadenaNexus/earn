How these are measured

Every number on this page comes from public sources and refreshes on its own. Nothing here is a forecast.

Throughput (TPS)

Transactions in all blocks created in the last 180 seconds, divided by 180. Refreshed every 10 seconds from the Chainweb GraphQL indexer.

First confirmation (TTFC)

Time from submitting a transaction until it is first included in a block. The median and 90th percentile are computed by the explorer's backend.

Active chains

A chain is active if it produced at least one block in the last 3 minutes.

Transactions, 24h

Successful transactions across all 20 chains over a rolling 24-hour window, with the per-chain split shown in the Chains section.

Transaction mix

The latest 500 transactions grouped by the first contract function they call. "Throughput without it" scales current TPS by the share of everything except the top function.

Nodes

A public crawler probes known Chainweb peers. "Behind tip" compares each node's cut height with the highest one seen; uptime is the share of probes that answered.
